# SynStudios Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 7, 2026  
**App:** SynStudios (`com.synstudios.preview`)

## Overview

SynStudios is an animation trial preview tool for 2D sprite sheets and 3D rigs. We designed the app to work offline-first on your device.

## Data we collect

SynStudios does **not** require an account. The app may store locally on your device:

- Animator reviewer name (optional, typed by you)
- Trial performance metrics (FPS, frame drops) during 30-second preview runs
- Uploaded sprite sheet files you choose to load (processed in-app, not uploaded to our servers by default)

## Data we do not collect

- No advertising identifiers
- No location tracking
- No contact list or SMS access
- No sale of personal data

## Permissions

The Android app requests only what is needed for preview:

- **Internet (optional):** for optional team sync when you enable live collaboration on the same network
- **Storage / media read:** to load sprite sheet PNG files you select

## Third-party services

If you use optional live sync, Socket.io may transmit trial start/complete events and reviewer names you enter. No third-party analytics SDKs are embedded in the production build.

## Children's privacy

SynStudios is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Contact

For privacy questions: support@synstudios.app

## Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
